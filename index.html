🙋About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Computer Science at Wuhan University. My research focuses on medical image analysis, medical world models, and intelligent analysis of fetal medical images.

I am particularly interested in ultrasound imaging, segmentation, landmark detection, and multimodal learning.
